# Surface brief: index.html (landing)

Scope: StruoLab public landing page. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: owners of traditional Colombian SMEs running on Excel, WhatsApp and paper. Action: request a free diagnosis (form handoff by email, WhatsApp when a number exists). Proof: an interactive before/after demonstration with clearly labeled illustrative data, the method, draft guarantees, the founders; no invented cases or metrics.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS; open placeholders (partners 2 and 3, email, WhatsApp, guarantees) stay visibly provisional; form has no backend.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Before and after. The page proves the change instead of describing it: the visitor marks what wastes their time and watches their own mess turn into order. Refuses the calm SaaS template and the previous kraft "bodega" costume.

OWN-WORLD: Cobalt #1E3BFF owns whole regions, acid lime #D7FF3A is reserved for action, white and ink #0B0B0F for reading. Bricolage Grotesque at extreme scale contrast (display up to ~8rem against 18px body), JetBrains Mono only for spreadsheet and data cells. Pill buttons with a sliding arrow. Blocks of unequal size, no icon tiles, no gradients, no glow.

STORY: The visitor reads the promise, marks their pains, sees a before/after of exactly that pain, understands what gets built and the four-step method, compares against agency and freelancer, meets the builders, and sends the order.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed cobalt. Giant white headline "Sacamos tu negocio del Excel." with "Excel" in lime struck through by a drawn line; one sentence of offer; lime pill "Pedir diagnóstico gratis" and an outline pill to the demo. The top edge of the white demo band shows below.

FORM: User-pinned direction C "Antes / después" from the three-option proposal (moderna, atrevida, impactante); supersedes roll 6051ae76. Signature interaction: the pain selector drives a before/after panel that wipes from chaos to order (clip-path), with process numbers revealing on scroll as the secondary moment.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
