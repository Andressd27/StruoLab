# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (existing `index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`), no framework or build step.

## Users

Owners and managers of traditional small and medium businesses in Colombia (commerce, logistics, services, health and similar) that still run operations on Excel, WhatsApp and paper. They are evaluating whether to hire someone to build or improve their software, usually without a technical background, and need to trust that the team will understand their business and not overcharge or disappear.

## Product Purpose

StruoLab is a three-founder technology studio that builds custom software, automates processes, integrates tools and delivers data dashboards so these businesses operate faster and with less manual work. The website is the company's public face: it must explain what they do, earn trust, and get the visitor to request a free initial consultation.

## Positioning

A small team where the client talks directly with the people who build the solution, with clear scope and price per stage, demos every two weeks, full ownership of code and data, and support after launch. Name origin: *Struo* is Latin for "I build"; *Lab* is the workshop where solutions are tested and built.

## Operating Context

- Primary market: Colombia; Spanish (Colombian register, "tú").
- Contact channels expected by the audience: WhatsApp first, then email and a contact form.
- Sales motion: free initial consultation, then a proposal with defined stages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: custom web/mobile software, process automation, data and BI dashboards, cloud and integrations (ERP, CRM, payment gateways), applied AI, technology consulting.
- Process: discovery, design, development in short cycles with demos, launch and support.
- Open decisions (do not present as final): founders 2 and 3 names and roles, real email/WhatsApp/city, commitments (24 h response, fixed quote per stage, 3 months of support) pending confirmation, contact form backend not connected.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: StruoLab (wordmark "Struo" + "Lab").
- Current logo: an "S" built from stacked blocks (`assets/logo.svg`); open to refinement within the "building blocks" idea.

## Evidence on Hand

- One or two real projects exist that could be shown; details, screenshots and permissions not yet provided.
- No testimonials, client logos, metrics or case studies exist. Never fabricate them; illustrative examples must be labeled as examples.

## Product Principles

1. Speak the business owner's language: processes, time and money, not technology jargon.
2. Show how the work happens instead of claiming quality.
3. Transparency over promises: scope, price and ownership are explicit.
4. Closeness: real people, direct contact, Colombian context.
